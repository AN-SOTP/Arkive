Outfit Swap gets its second and third passes and a way to keep an outfit's own materials, the Sounds tab works again after the 2026-10-21 game update, and the Mods list, Apply and startup are much faster.

- Outfit Swap fitting: bare skin takes the new body's shape and skin layout, the left and right sides fit the same way, the new body's own hands and fingers are used where the outfit leaves them bare, feet and shoes keep their shape, see-through cloth stays see-through, the neck meets the head, colours and sheen are closer to the original, and back panels hang from the back instead of following the arm. In tests between female bodies (Warrior, Assassin, Mage, Martial Artist) most swaps no longer show the faults 0.5.6 had. Swaps can be saved: the bone-use checks SMA010 and SMA011 only warn for swapped outfits (other mesh edits still block on them). The first swap for each pair of bodies relearns its fit, which takes about a minute.
- Outfit Swap: "Keep the outfit's own materials (experimental)" draws the outfit with its own game materials (sheen, see-through cloth and glow included) instead of painting it into the replaced outfit's pictures. The mod also changes the item looks in data4.lpk that draw the replaced outfit, colour variants included, and the outfit's own package must stay in the game.
- Outfit Swap: a class box above each list shows one class's skins and its body's outfits; right-click an outfit to open it in the Asset Viewer; a wait card shows while a swap preview opens.
- Asset Library: class filters include the classes built on a body (Female Warrior finds Slayer and Valkyrie skins, such as the Eternity outfits); map results and map cards no longer appear when a class filter is set or the asset type is not one maps hold; launch class skins such as Cold Fury and Burning Rage are found by their in-game names.
- Sounds: the Sounds tab loads again after the 2026-10-21 game update, which added duplicate loose sound files. Replacing music can fit the sound bank to the new file's length ("Fit sound bank to new length", on by default), so a longer or shorter song plays through and loops instead of being padded or cut.
- Mods: Remove mod is now Delete mod and is on the right-click menu too. A disabled mod whose files stay in the game until the next Apply says so ("Disabled · still in game until Apply").
- Faster: starting Arkive checks changed game files once per package and remembers the answer (about a minute less on a large library after a game update); enabling, disabling and priority changes take 2-4 s instead of 7-11 s; the review before Apply takes seconds instead of most of a minute.

Known issues: a Summoner outfit swapped onto another body can show silver toes in the open world. Dresses can come out slightly less bright than the original. Swaps onto male bodies and the Specialist are rougher than between other female bodies.
